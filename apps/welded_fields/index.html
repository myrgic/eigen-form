Shape count
holes (β₁)– pieces (β₀)– bright share–

Counts pieces and holes in the bright part on the wrap-around sheet (a torus), so shapes that cross an edge are counted once. A band that wraps all the way round counts as a hole too. Checked against an independent calculation on 924 test masks.

How long things last
scent: frames to fade to half– arrows: frames to fade to half– arrows ÷ scent lifetime– outside noise: frames per change– noise ÷ scent lifetime– frames per second–

Closed loop: with outside noise at 0, the dots are pushed only by arrows they wrote themselves. The page guesses that "arrows ÷ scent lifetime" then controls the patterns. That guess hasn't been tested: in a review, runs with the same ratio did not behave alike. At the default arrow settings the effect on the dots' headings is too weak to detect.
