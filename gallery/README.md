# gallery/: research gallery (generated)

The eigen-form landing page (`../index.html`) renders this directory. Every simulation in
the lab sits beside the equations it implements, the papers those equations come from,
and an essay comparing the paper with the code.

- `data.json`, `papers/`, `posters/` and `vendor/katex/` are GENERATED by the cog
  substrate's `gallery_build.py`, from the simulation and equation registries. Do not
  hand-edit them.
- `articles/*.md` are the essays, in source form. Each quotes papers only from full
  texts actually read, with page numbers, and cites code by file:line at a pinned commit.
- Full-text PDFs are hosted ONLY for public-domain and CC-BY works: Plummer 1911,
  Rutherford and Soddy 1903, Uhlenbeck and Ornstein 1930, Voorhees 1999 (US Government
  work) and Jones 2010 (CC-BY). Other sources are linked (free copies) or cited
  (paywalled papers and books).
- KaTeX (MIT): `vendor/katex/LICENSE`.
