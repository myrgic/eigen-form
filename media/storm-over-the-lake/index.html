Storm over the Lake

Reaction-diffusion, played by the score. Myrgic Labs.

The picture is a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion field: two chemicals on a grid, and two numbers that decide whether they make mazes, holes or chaos. Nothing here follows the sound by listening to it. The song was written in Oscine, and the field is played by the same score the synths are: every note seeds the field where its pitch puts it, the kick stirs it, and each section of the song has its own chemistry.

Because the score is known ahead of time, the field can act before things happen. Through the last bar of every section the stage empties, and on the downbeat of the next one a flower is drawn and the new chemistry grows out of it.
